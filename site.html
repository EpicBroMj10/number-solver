Our mission

Don't blend into the wallpaper

We exist to fuel your passion for standout fashion, or at least to stop you blending into the wallpaper. Our ridiculously cool, well-made clothes raise your style without raising your stress.

We believe everyone deserves high-quality clothes at the right price, because overpriced fashion should be a crime. This isn't just a store. It's your way into a wardrobe that turns heads, starts conversations and occasionally confuses people (in a good way).

Looking for curated coolness? Congrats, you found NotTheBestPlaceToBuyStuff. Not the best place to buy stuff, but definitely the best place to be awesome.

We're a new startup obsessed with giving you the best shopping experience we can. We keep improving our range, our prices and our customer service. Thanks for choosing us.
